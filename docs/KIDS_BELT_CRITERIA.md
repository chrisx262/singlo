# Singlo Kids Belt Promotion Criteria

This guide defines what a player ages 5-12 should practice and master before advancing through the Singlo belt system.

The goal is to make Singlo a real vocal development game without making it feel like school, therapy, or a test. The game should reward effort during practice, but belt promotion should represent demonstrated mastery.

Black Belt should mean the player is near-professional for their age band: not adult-professional, not formally certified, but clearly stage-ready compared with typical peers in the same age range.

## Curriculum Anchors

Use beginner singing curricula as inspiration, not as a formal certification claim.

Useful reference points:

- [School of Rock Singing Lessons](https://www.schoolofrock.com/music-lessons/singing-lessons): beginner voice work emphasizes breathing, posture, diction, pitch training, timbre, steady tempo, and performance.
- [Trinity Rock & Pop Vocals Initial](https://www.trinityrock.com/instruments/vocals/initial): early vocal assessment includes breath control, diction, rhythm, set-list performance, playback/improvising, and technical focus songs.

Professional beginner vocal programs commonly emphasize:

- posture and comfortable setup
- breathing and breath control
- diction and vowels
- pitch awareness
- steady tempo and rhythm
- tone and timbre
- call-and-response / playback
- short performances

Singlo translates those skills into voice-controlled game tasks.

## The Kids Advancement Rule

Each belt uses an 11-step promotion model:

- 7 Practice Sparks: the player tries the skill in playful mode. These count effort, not perfection.
- 3 Mastery Stars: the player completes measured challenges with age-appropriate thresholds.
- 1 Show Pass: the player completes a short finale that combines the belt skill with earlier skills.

Promotion should feel like:

1. Discover the sound.
2. Practice the sound.
3. Control the sound.
4. Use the sound in a tiny performance.

The game should never punish a child for a messy voice day. If a player does not meet a mastery threshold, Singlo should keep the scene alive and invite another try.

## Mastery Philosophy

Belts are not participation trophies. Practice Sparks, XP, cosmetics, and streak rewards can celebrate effort, but they should not advance a player to the next belt by themselves.

Promotion requires:

- completing all 7 Practice Sparks for that belt
- earning all 3 Mastery Stars for that belt
- passing the Show Pass for that belt
- showing the skill more than once, so promotion is based on consistency instead of one lucky attempt

The first code version can implement a simpler version of this, but the product direction should stay clear: XP measures activity, belts measure ability.

## Age-Band Mastery Standard

Singlo should judge mastery against the player's age band.

| Age Band | Black Belt Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ages 5-7 | Can complete short voice games with intentional sound, safe volume, pitch direction, simple rhythm, and a confident 20-30 second stage moment. |
| Ages 8-12 | Can perform a 45-60 second age-appropriate song excerpt or vocal routine with controlled breath, mostly accurate pitch, steady rhythm, clear vowels, dynamics, and stage confidence. |

The player should not need adult vocal power or adult emotional interpretation. The target is age-appropriate control, musicality, confidence, and safe technique.

## Age-Adaptive Thresholds

These are starting targets to test, not permanent rules.

| Skill Metric | Ages 5-7 | Ages 8-12 | Bonus / Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pitch match | within 200 cents or correct contour | within 100 cents for 1 second | within 50 cents |
| Sustained sound | 3 seconds continuous voice | 5 seconds continuous voice | 8 seconds relaxed voice |
| Rhythm timing | 6 of 8 hits within 350 ms | 8 of 10 hits within 250 ms | within 150 ms |
| Siren / pitch glide | move at least 3 semitones | move at least 5 semitones | smooth low-high-low glide |
| Melody chunk | match 3-note shape | match 4-note shape plus rough rhythm | match pitch and rhythm |
| Dynamics | show soft and strong voice | soft, medium, strong voice | smooth crescendo / decrescendo |

Later belts should reuse earlier skills at a higher consistency standard. For example, Yellow Belt may allow a forgiving pitch window, but Black Belt should expect the player to combine pitch, rhythm, breath, diction, and dynamics in one performance.

Humming should count for early belts. Singing syllables and words can become more important later, but the first kids loop should not require a child to sing lyrics.

## Vocal Health Rules

The game should encourage safe voice use:

- No shouting as a required mechanic.
- If input clips or gets too loud, reward the attempt but coach a softer sound.
- Keep early sessions short.
- Add rest prompts during longer sessions.
- Avoid saying the player has a "bad" voice.
- Use "try softer," "try smoother," and "let the sound float" instead of negative feedback.

## Belt Ladder

### White Belt: Voice Activation

Core skill: intentional sound, microphone comfort, and start/stop control.

Game task: wake up the stage with any sound, hum, spoken sound, or sung note.

Practice Sparks:

- Make any sound 7 times and see the stage react.
- Tap a piano key and make a sound after hearing it.
- Try sound at least once without needing pitch accuracy.

Mastery Stars:

- Create 10 clear sound events above the room noise level.
- Start sound after a cue at least 5 times.
- Stop sound after a cue at least 3 times.

Show Pass:

- Complete a 15-second stage wake-up where sound triggers lights at least 5 times.

Tech scoring:

- Microphone permission.
- Adaptive noise floor.
- Volume / energy trigger.
- Basic onset and silence detection.

Mastery meaning: the player can intentionally make the game react with their voice.

### Yellow Belt: Pitch Awareness

Core skill: hearing a target note and moving the voice toward it.

Game task: tap or hear one note, then hum or sing toward that note until the stage brightens.

Practice Sparks:

- Try 7 target-note echoes.
- Watch the pitch trail move up and down.
- Earn credit for exploring high, middle, and low sounds.

Mastery Stars:

- Match 3 different target notes within the age-adaptive pitch threshold.
- Hold each matched note for about 1 second.
- Complete at least 5 successful matches across 10 attempts.

Show Pass:

- Match 3 glowing notes one at a time in a short stage round.

Tech scoring:

- Pitch detection.
- Cents difference from target note.
- Forgiving match window.
- Piano reference notes.

Mastery meaning: the player understands that their voice can move toward a musical target.

### Orange Belt: Steady Sound And Breath

Core skill: relaxed sustained voice.

Game task: hold a hum or note to keep a spotlight, color stream, or visual effect alive.

Practice Sparks:

- Try 7 held sounds.
- Fill the visual meter by keeping any comfortable sound going.
- Humming counts.

Mastery Stars:

- Hold 3 relaxed sounds for the age-adaptive sustain threshold.
- Keep volume from dropping out during the hold.
- Avoid clipping or shouting during the hold.

Show Pass:

- Hold a note or hum long enough to complete one 15-second stage effect sequence.

Tech scoring:

- Continuous voice detection.
- Hold duration.
- Volume stability.
- Optional pitch stability bonus.

Mastery meaning: the player can keep sound steady without forcing it.

### Green Belt: Range Exploration

Core skill: safe low-to-high and high-to-low voice movement.

Game task: slide the voice like a siren to move a visual path from low to high.

Practice Sparks:

- Try 7 sirens or pitch slides.
- Explore low, middle, and high areas.
- Earn credit for movement, not perfect notes.

Mastery Stars:

- Complete 5 glides that move by the age-adaptive semitone target.
- Complete at least one low-to-high glide.
- Complete at least one high-to-low glide.

Show Pass:

- Use pitch slides to light three stage zones: low, middle, and high.

Tech scoring:

- Pitch contour.
- Lowest and highest detected pitch.
- Semitone movement.
- Smoothness bonus.

Mastery meaning: the player can explore range safely and understand pitch direction.

### Blue Belt: Rhythm And Short Sounds

Core skill: timing, onsets, and short controlled sounds.

Game task: make short sounds in time with a beat to trigger stage hits.

Practice Sparks:

- Try 7 rhythm rounds with voice, hum pulses, claps, or spoken sounds.
- Copy simple two-beat patterns.
- Learn that silence between sounds matters.

Mastery Stars:

- Hit the age-adaptive rhythm target in 3 short rounds.
- Produce clear short sounds with space between them.
- Copy at least one two-beat pattern.

Show Pass:

- Complete a 15-second beat round with stage hits on time.

Tech scoring:

- Onset detection.
- Timing against beat grid.
- Short sound duration.
- Silence gaps.

Mastery meaning: the player can use the voice as a rhythmic instrument.

### Purple Belt: Vowels And Diction

Core skill: shaping sound into clear vowel patterns.

Game task: sing or speak vowel chains such as "ah, eh, ee, oh, oo" to open paths or change stage colors.

Practice Sparks:

- Try 7 vowel chains.
- Use spoken or sung voice.
- Humming no longer counts for every prompt, but it can still earn effort credit.

Mastery Stars:

- Complete 5 timed vowel prompts with voice present through each vowel.
- Hold each vowel for at least 0.5 seconds.
- Complete one full "ah, eh, ee, oh, oo" chain.

Show Pass:

- Perform a short vowel pattern that changes the stage through five colors.

Tech scoring:

- V1: prompt timing plus voice energy.
- V2: broad vowel-shape estimate using spectral/formant features.
- Optional parent/coach listen-back before using strict auto-grading.

Mastery meaning: the player can shape voice into intentional speech/singing sounds.

### Red Belt: Melody Chunks

Core skill: musical memory and Gestalt-friendly phrase copying.

Game task: hear a tiny musical phrase, then copy the shape with humming, singing, or a comfortable syllable.

Practice Sparks:

- Try 7 call-and-response phrases.
- Match contour before exact pitch.
- Use 3-note chunks before longer phrases.

Mastery Stars:

- Match 3 short melody chunks using the age-adaptive contour threshold.
- Keep the phrase in the right general rhythm.
- Complete at least one phrase after only one listen.

Show Pass:

- Copy a 3- or 4-note stage phrase that becomes a mini hook.

Tech scoring:

- Pitch contour: up, down, same.
- Rough interval size.
- Phrase timing.
- Exact pitch bonus.

Mastery meaning: the player can copy musical chunks, not just isolated notes.

### Brown Belt: Dynamics And Expression

Core skill: controlled soft, medium, and strong voice.

Game task: change vocal energy to move lights, color, and stage motion without shouting.

Practice Sparks:

- Try 7 dynamic changes.
- Explore soft and strong sounds.
- Learn that louder is not always better.

Mastery Stars:

- Complete 5 soft-to-strong or strong-to-soft changes.
- Show at least two clear volume levels.
- Avoid clipping during strong sounds.

Show Pass:

- Perform a short expressive round with soft, medium, and strong moments.

Tech scoring:

- RMS / volume level.
- Relative change from player's own baseline.
- Clipping detection.
- Smoothness bonus.

Mastery meaning: the player can add expression and control intensity.

### Black Belt: Age-Band Stage Ready

Core skill: combine pitch, breath, rhythm, range, diction, melody, expression, and confidence into an age-appropriate performance.

Game task: perform a grand finale that creates a local music-video-style recap or performance card.

Practice Sparks:

- Complete 7 rehearsal rounds that revisit earlier belt skills.
- Choose a simple prompt, melody, or stage pattern.
- Practice without needing a perfect score.

Mastery Stars:

- Complete a pitch task and stay mostly in the correct musical shape.
- Complete a rhythm or melody task with clear timing.
- Complete a sustain or dynamics task with safe, controlled volume.

Show Pass:

- Ages 5-7: finish a 20-30 second performance with at least 3 of the 4 core systems active: pitch direction, rhythm, sustain, expression.
- Ages 8-12: finish a 45-60 second performance with at least 4 of the 5 core systems active: pitch, rhythm, sustain, diction, expression.
- Complete the Black Belt Show Pass successfully on at least 3 separate sessions before promotion.

Tech scoring:

- Combined skill checklist.
- Performance completion and consistency across sessions.
- Local-only recap card.
- No raw audio upload by default.

Mastery meaning: the player is stage-ready for their age band and has the foundation for richer songs, longer shows, or the next age track.

## Implementation Notes

- XP can still exist, but belt promotion must require skill gates.
- A player can earn XP for effort without advancing belts.
- Practice Sparks can unlock visual rewards, but Mastery Stars and Show Passes unlock belt promotion.
- The first implementation only needs White and Yellow gates.
- Light Up The Stage with Sound Painting should support White, Yellow, and Orange mechanics first.
- Keep thresholds configurable so we can tune after watching real kids play.
- Store progress locally at first.
- Do not create public kids leaderboards until privacy and parent controls are designed.
